Yes. The converter uses Microsoft Word to render each document, which keeps formatting, fonts, tables and images identical to the original. Word must be installed and licensed on the PC running the converter.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion happens entirely on your own computer. Your files are never uploaded to a server or third-party service.
